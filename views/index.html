The Real Picture

It's not your fault.
But it does become your problem.

Most website accessibility issues stem from templates built before modern standards, plugins that inject inaccessible code, or agencies that simply didn't prioritize it. You inherited the problem. We help you fix it.

1 in 4 adults in the US lives with a disability.

That's over 61 million people who encounter barriers on inaccessible websites every single day. When your site can't be navigated by keyboard, read by a screen reader, or understood by someone with cognitive differences, you're closing the door on a quarter of your potential customers.

ADA lawsuits are at an all-time high.

Courts continue to extend ADA protections to private business websites under Title III, with WCAG 2.2 Level AA as the standard they reference. (The DOJ's separate Title II rule covers state and local government sites.) Accessibility isn't a courtesy — it's legal infrastructure. We help you get ahead of it before a demand letter arrives.

AI search engines can't cite what they can't read.

ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews parse your site's structure to decide if you're a credible source worth recommending. "Div soup" — pages built with no semantic landmarks — is invisible to these systems. Clean, accessible code is the same code that gets your business cited as an authoritative answer.

      Common Questions

      Questions we answer every week

      Straight answers to what business owners ask us most. Want more? Read the full FAQ.

      Does my small business website need to be ADA compliant?

      Most private businesses that serve the public fall under ADA Title III, and courts increasingly apply it to websites. (Title II is separate — it covers state and local government entities like schools and courts.) We help you meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA, the standard courts reference, which reduces legal risk and opens your site to more customers.

      What is AEO, and why does it matter for my business?

      AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. We structure your content so AI search engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews — read, understand, and recommend your business when people ask questions. We build the clean, semantic structure these systems cite as authoritative.

      Should I hire a consultant or use an accessibility overlay tool?

      We fix the real thing. Overlay widgets patch the surface in the browser but leave the underlying barriers in place, and the disability community widely criticizes them. We address root causes in your code and content, so your site works with every assistive technology.

      How is an accessibility audit different from ongoing monitoring?

      An audit is a point-in-time assessment that identifies existing barriers. Monitoring is continuous — we re-scan your site regularly to catch new issues as you add content, update plugins, or change templates. The audit gets you to a baseline; monitoring keeps you there.
